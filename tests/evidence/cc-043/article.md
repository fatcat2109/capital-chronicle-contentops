# Capital Chronicle's ECI record: a smaller quarterly rise

Capital Chronicle's retained Employment Cost Index record says civilian compensation rose 0.8% in the third quarter of 2026, after 0.9% in the second. The record attributes the figures to the U.S. Bureau of Labor Statistics (BLS). Fixture, not current data.

## What the release record says

On the seasonally adjusted, three-month measures, civilian wages and salaries rose 0.7%, while benefits rose 0.9%. Their preceding readings were 0.9% and 1.0%, respectively.

The 12-month civilian-compensation rate was 3.4%, unchanged from the prior 3.4%. That measure is not seasonally adjusted, unlike the quarterly measures.
