## 1. Owner decision

Capital Chronicle ContentOps must move into useful real daily operation as quickly as safely possible.

The current objective is not to perfect every platform, UI surface, source adapter, or infrastructure layer before launch. The objective is to establish one repeatable evidence-governed Daily Live newsroom loop, operate it on real current news, learn from production, and finish Tier-1 from production evidence.

The accelerated route is:

```text
current canonical newsroom
→ close the remaining story/evidence wiring blockers
→ one fresh current live canary
→ 5–10 operating days of Daily Live probation
→ build/attach Capital Chronicle social surfaces in parallel
→ minimal final operator-UI rebuild from real live states
→ Daily Live acceptance + new immutable release identity
→ begin ContentOps V2 Pro Video Factory
```

Do not delay Daily Live for broad horizontal hardening, perfect support for every social platform, a perfect control-room redesign, five mandatory daily article windows, licensed-news breadth, or video.

## 2. Product thesis

Capital Chronicle is the intelligence product. ContentOps is its autonomous attention, distribution, audience-acquisition, and content-learning engine.

Capital Chronicle owns:

- economic and market analysis;
- micro/macro/global-macro reports;
- deterministic model calculations;
- scenarios and probabilistic views;
- Bayesian cases and updates;
- forecasts and regimes;
- numeric truth and realized-outcome attribution;
- analytical confidence, assumptions, limitations, and invalidation conditions.

ContentOps owns:

- current headline and primary-event discovery;
- duplicate/update-chain clustering;
- evidence, permission, freshness, and material-delta gates;
- editorial ranking and no-publication decisions;
- factual reporting from authorized evidence;
- framing, writing, editing, SEO, images, and deterministic charts from authorized inputs;
- native platform packages;
- publication control, strict readback, reconciliation, incidents, and recovery;
- qualified-audience acquisition and performance learning;
- faithful transformation of governed Capital Chronicle analysis packets.

ContentOps must never become a second Capital Chronicle analytical or numeric authority.

## 3. North-star operating loop

The Daily Live product is successful when the same canonical system repeatedly performs:

```text
fresh rolling news/headline universe
→ deterministic normalization and dedupe
→ hierarchical semantic clustering
→ compact global editorial ranking
→ exact story-type + article-mode routing
→ targeted authoritative evidence acquisition
→ first evidence-viable ranked story OR explicit abstention
→ grounded article / SEO / visual package
→ independent semantic review and bounded revision
→ platform-native packages
→ deterministic destination readiness
→ AUTONOMOUS_DEFAULT public writes only where exact gates pass
→ strict readback / reconciliation
→ measured qualified engagement and operating cost
→ next daily decision
```

A correct `NO_PUBLICATION` result is a successful newsroom decision.

## 4. Daily Live editorial products

Tier-1 Daily Live should prioritize four useful products rather than generic AI commentary.

### 4.1 Primary-source Event Brief

For court decisions, regulation, policy actions, sanctions, official announcements, filings, and other primary-source events.

ContentOps may report what the authoritative source establishes and explain bounded factual implications. It may not create unsupported market or macro conclusions.

### 4.2 Data Release Brief

ContentOps may report an official data release using exact authorized release values, timestamps, revisions, definitions, and historical context that is directly supported by approved evidence.

Deeper market, scenario, macro, probabilistic, or model interpretation remains Capital Chronicle authority.

### 4.3 Company / Sector Brief

ContentOps may report SEC filings, company releases, earnings/guidance, and material corporate events from authoritative sources.

A straight factual report must not require a Capital Chronicle market model merely because the event could affect markets. Analytical/valuation/market-impact extensions do require the appropriate governed Capital Chronicle authority.

### 4.4 Capital Chronicle Insight

Governed Capital Chronicle analysis is transformed faithfully into premium articles, charts, newsletters, social packages, and later video.

This is the differentiated intelligence layer. ContentOps may change presentation, not analytical substance.

## 5. Evidence doctrine: story type plus article mode

A story type alone is not sufficient to determine the evidence burden.

Current Tier-1 authority is:

```text
story type
+
article mode
→ exact evidence profile
```

Do not collapse factual reporting and analytical interpretation into one evidence requirement.

Examples:

```text
company_sector_event + straight_news
→ company filing/release + exact factual evidence

company_sector_event + analysis
→ company filing/release + sector context + required Capital Chronicle market authority


data_release + straight_news
→ official release + exact timestamps/values/definitions

data_release + analysis
→ official release + required Capital Chronicle analytical/market authority


regulatory_fiscal_event + straight_news
→ official document + implementation timeline + affected entities
```

A model-generated `market_sensitive` flag is editorial metadata. It does not independently grant or create Capital Chronicle numeric/analytical authority requirements.

Capital Chronicle authority is required only where the resolved evidence contract requires analytical/numeric/market authority.

X/social text is discovery and editorial-priority input only. It never establishes factual or numeric truth.

## 6. Launch source priorities

Do not build every evidence source before launch.

The highest-value initial official source families are:

1. `official_regulatory_fiscal`
2. `company_primary` / `sec_regulatory`
3. `official_macro`
4. existing exact Capital Chronicle market/analysis authority where required

Add other official families only when a real ranked story is blocked and the bounded adapter directly improves Daily Live utility.

Licensed-news breadth is not a v1 launch prerequisite.

## 7. Daily cadence

Initial production cadence:

- one scheduled core editorial decision per operating day;
- one optional material-event/breaking trigger when a genuinely new high-value event warrants it;
- no mandatory article quota;
- no requirement to publish five times per day.

Five decision windows remain a future capability, not a launch requirement.

First measure:

- cost per daily decision;
- cost per accepted package;
- runtime per accepted package;
- publication/abstention rate;
- evidence blocker distribution;
- operator time;
- article/review defects;
- subscriber/canonical-click/qualified-engagement outcomes.

Expand cadence only after evidence supports it.

## 8. Social distribution strategy

Social platform readiness must progress in parallel with Daily Live and must not block the canonical article/newsletter product.

For each destination, establish only:

```text
brand identity
→ account/profile binding
→ readiness
→ native package contract
→ publication
→ strict readback
→ basic performance metrics
```

At runtime, publish only to currently verified canonical `READY_AUTHENTICATED` or `READY_NON_BROWSER_BINDING` destinations. A blocked destination is skipped; it does not invalidate an otherwise publishable canonical article.

Substack remains the current canonical long-form/newsletter surface, but Capital Chronicle brand/content identity and subscriber lineage must remain portable and independent of any single platform.

## 9. Daily Live probation

After one fresh successful canary, stop opening architecture work by default and operate the product for approximately 5–10 operating days.

The probation should include:

- real current news decisions;
- several real publications when evidence warrants them;
- explicit no-publication outcomes;
- more than one story type/source family when eligible;
- strict public readback for every public object;
- zero unreconciled unknown writes;
- measured provider/token/runtime cost;
- measured operator intervention;
- observed content defects and evidence blockers;
- initial subscriber/canonical-click/platform observations.

Do not make large learning-policy changes from tiny samples.

## 11. UI strategy

Do not block Daily Live on a major speculative UI redesign.

Run production first. Then rebuild/polish `ui/contentops_v5/` around real states such as:

- provider degraded/unavailable;
- evidence incomplete/stale;
- `NO_PUBLICATION`;
- story selected;
- review revision;
- destination ready/blocked;
- published;
- readback complete;
- unknown write;
- reconciliation;
- cost/runtime;
- performance observations.

Visible browser acceptance remains screenshot-based when the actual UI rebuild occurs.

## 12. ContentOps V2 — Pro Video Factory

Video is the immediate post-Daily-Live product expansion, not a Tier-1 launch blocker.

The existing Tier-2 Pro Video Factory North Star remains compatible and is now the intended `v2.0.0` direction after the Daily Live baseline is accepted and frozen.

V2 must reuse the same canonical selected story, evidence, permissions, Capital Chronicle analytical authority, durable state, publication authority, and performance identity.

- `LONG_FORM_EDITORIAL_15_45M`

V2 must not create a second newsroom or analytical authority.

## 13. Fast-ship doctrine

Prioritize user-visible product value and production learning.

Stop only for substantive product/safety blockers such as secret exposure, fabricated numeric truth, unauthorized access/write, destructive mutation, protected-release mutation, irreconcilable remote mismatch, unresolved merge conflict, or required external/operator input that cannot be safely inferred.

On a substantive problem, stop immediately and report only the exact issue, last successful stage, public/unknown-write state, and what is needed to continue.

Do not create closure ceremony, audit-of-audit loops, broad test runs, or documentation work solely to mark a task complete.
