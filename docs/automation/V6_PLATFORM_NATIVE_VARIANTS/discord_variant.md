**How Maritime Chokepoints and Monetary Policy Realign Global Capital**

Hey everyone! We've just published a deep-dive macroeconomic analysis looking at the collision between global logistics and central bank policy. 

**Key Takeaways:**
* **The Ton-Mile Effect:** Rerouting ships around Africa adds 10–14 days to voyages, effectively shrinking global shipping capacity and driving up spot rates with a 6-to-9-month lag before hitting CPI.
* **The Central Bank Dilemma:** Traditional monetary tools manage demand, not supply-side shocks. Raising rates to fight shipping-driven inflation risks over-tightening highly leveraged sectors.
* **Corporate Divergence:** Mega-caps are securing long-term supply deals (like Broadcom's Apple deal through 2031) to hedge risks, while micro-caps (like FOXO Technologies) are forced into rapid operational restructurings to preserve liquidity.
* **The Green Energy Paradox:** Geopolitical oil disruptions highlight the need for renewables, yet the high-interest-rate response makes capital-intensive green projects much more expensive to finance.
