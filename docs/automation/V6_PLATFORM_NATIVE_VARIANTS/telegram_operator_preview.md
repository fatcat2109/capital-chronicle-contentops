⚓️ **The Friction of Distance: How Maritime Chokepoints and Monetary Policy Realign Global Capital**

The modern global economy’s assumption of frictionless transit is breaking down. Geopolitical bottlenecks in critical maritime corridors are forcing ships to bypass key canals, triggering the "ton-mile effect" and driving up global logistics costs.

This structural shift complicates the mission of central banks. Because monetary policy is designed to manage demand, using benchmark rates to fight supply-side inflation risks triggering economic slowdowns while doing nothing to clear port congestion.
