Capital Chronicle owns analytical/numeric truth: economic and market analysis, micro/macro/global-macro reports, model calculations, scenarios/probabilities, Bayesian outputs, forecasts/regimes, numeric truth, realized outcomes, and analytical error attribution.

## 6. Always-on runtime doctrine

Always-on means a low-cost persistent supervisor, not continuous model calls.

Idle duties include durable-state health, next-window calculation, intake freshness, material-event trigger state, due readback/reconciliation, due metrics observations, learning-policy evaluation, incidents, and kill switch.

Expensive newsroom/model work wakes only when a scheduled/editorial or material-event decision is due.

Every editorial window requires deterministic identity/idempotency so duplicate ticks/restart cannot produce duplicate cycles.

## 7. Scheduling / performance / learning

The old one-decision-per-day cadence is a bootstrap policy.

The final app maintains a versioned editorial-window policy and may improve timing, SEO, editorial priority, content/package strategy, and platform-specific distribution from real qualified-engagement observations.

Learning may never change evidence, permissions, Capital Chronicle analytical output, factual/numeric truth, account identity, or safety/publication gates.

Small samples must yield no-op/low-confidence outcomes rather than major automatic changes.
