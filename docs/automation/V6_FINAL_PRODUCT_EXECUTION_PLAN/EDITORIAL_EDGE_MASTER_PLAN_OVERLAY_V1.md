## Editorial product objective

Every warranted article should make the following easy to understand where evidence permits:

- what changed;
- how large/material it is relative to the relevant baseline;
- why it matters;
- the physical, institutional, financial, or policy mechanism;
- the countervailing force or uncertainty;
- what evidence would confirm/challenge the interpretation;
- what an informed reader should watch next.

The product should feel more analytical than generic wire copy, more disciplined than finance-influencer commentary, and more accessible than an internal research memo.

## SEO product objective

SEO supports durable discovery without changing truth or creating filler. Optimize useful titles, metadata, search intent, internal links, and structured data only around a warranted article. Do not manufacture an article for a keyword.

## Operating economics

Preserve the low-call architecture:

- zero model calls when no governed evidence warrants semantic work;
- one bounded research synthesis only when documents exist and synthesis is required;
- exactly one fresh isolated XHIGH final writer for a warranted article;
- zero mandatory semantic-review calls for ordinary reporting;
- bounded deterministic checks;
- no fixed media quota;
- zero media remains valid.
