U.S. Retail Sales Post First Decline in Nine Months

Cheaper gas and an “Amazon Prime hangover” were identified as the chief culprits, while the exact size of the decline remains unspecified.

U.S. retail sales declined in July, the first drop in nine months, Reuters reported.

MarketWatch identified cheaper gas and an “Amazon Prime hangover” as the chief culprits behind the slump. CNN separately reported that U.S. consumers cut retail spending last month.

The exact percentage or dollar amount of the decline is not specified, leaving the scale of the pullback unresolved.
