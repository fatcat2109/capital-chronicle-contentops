## Editorial inference controls

- The thesis that refunds temporarily supported spring spending is presented as analysis corroborated by AP, not a direct IRS finding.
- The article does not calculate “real retail sales” by subtracting CPI.
- The article does not infer gasoline volumes from station receipts.
- Policy implications are conditional analysis. No rate move is predicted as a fact.
- The only pullquote beside the official portrait is labeled `CAPITAL CHRONICLE ANALYSIS — OUR VIEW` and the caption says the analysis is Capital Chronicle’s own.
