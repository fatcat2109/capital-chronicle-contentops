## Interpretation boundaries

- IRS figures measure refunds issued, not what recipients spent or when they spent it. The article therefore describes refunds as a plausible, temporary cash-flow tailwind corroborated by contemporaneous reporting, not as a mechanically estimated contribution to retail sales.
- Census retail sales are nominal, seasonally adjusted estimates and exclude most services. The article does not subtract CPI from retail sales or label the result “real retail sales.”
- Category changes are not used to infer physical volumes. In particular, gasoline-station receipts are not translated into gallons sold.
- The “control group” figure is taken from AP’s description of the standard measure and is not presented as a separate Census headline series.
