# Capital Chronicle ContentOps — Final Product Scope Overlay V2

Authority date: 2026-08-06

This overlay is current product-direction authority. It supersedes conflicting product-scope and operating-mode language in older institutional and V6 plans while preserving verified historical evidence, immutable release artifacts, safety rules, and accepted executable boundaries.

## 1. Product ownership boundary

### Capital Chronicle main project owns

- daily economic and market analysis;
- microeconomic, macroeconomic, and global-macro reports;
- scenarios and probabilistic views;
- deterministic model calculations;
- Bayesian priors, evidence updates, likelihood assumptions, and posteriors;
- market-regime and forecast authority;
- numeric truth, realized outcomes, error metrics, and model attribution.

### ContentOps owns

- headlines, breaking news, and business-news intake;
- event clustering, duplicate detection, corrections, and update chains;
- evidence, permission, freshness, and material-delta gates;
- editorial ranking, daily portfolio selection, and no-publication decisions;
- content mode, angle, audience, and framing;
- reporting, writing, editing, SEO, images, charts, and platform-native packages;
- publication control, readback, reconciliation, and incident handling;
- content-performance observation and bounded packaging/selection learning;
- faithful transformation of governed Capital Chronicle analysis packets.

ContentOps must not create authoritative scenarios, model outputs, Bayesian probabilities, economic forecasts, market regimes, or numeric truth. A ContentOps chart may visualize authorized data and calculations; it may not originate analytical authority.

## 2. Final product definition

Capital Chronicle ContentOps is an autonomous AI-native newsroom and content factory with two input lanes:

```text
NEWSROOM LANE
fresh governed headlines and primary-source events
→ cluster, rank, select or abstain
→ report, explain, optimize, visualize, package, publish, read back, learn

CAPITAL CHRONICLE LANE
governed analysis packet
→ validate exact lineage and permission
→ transform faithfully into reports, articles, visuals, and native packages
→ publish, read back, and measure content performance
```

The product must support a diversified content universe including U.S. equities and Big Tech, sector trends, economic releases, political and policy news, central banks, rates and credit, FX and commodities, energy and materials, geopolitics, trade and supply chains, regulation and law, global corporate events, and Capital Chronicle analysis products.

## 3. Operating modes

- `AUTONOMOUS_DEFAULT`: default product mode; public writes occur only when exact deterministic gates pass.
- `SUPERVISED_OPERATOR_GATE`: optional toggle that pauses before public write.
- `SHADOW_ONLY`: full production cycle with zero public writes.
- `KILL_SWITCH`: blocks new public writes while allowing readback, reconciliation, and recovery.

Older universal mandatory-approval language is superseded. Historical supervised release evidence remains valid historical evidence.

## 4. Build doctrine

Use FAST SHIP + CORE V0.

Prioritize the smallest complete product loop over additional horizontal schemas, evidence ceremony, platform expansion, or infrastructure perfection. Reuse accepted components. Add durable-state, approval, outbox, scheduler, provider, or UI work only when it directly blocks the final vertical slice or launch gate.

The final vertical slice must prove:

- diversified news intake and clustering;
- one selected news package or explicit abstention;
- one governed Capital Chronicle analysis transformation;
- editorial, SEO, image/chart, and platform-native production;
- exact review and authority fidelity;
- shadow-mode evidence and canonical UI visibility;
- later, a tightly authorized live cohort with strict readback.
