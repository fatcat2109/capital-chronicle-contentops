## 1. Build-phase media envelope

Current proof/build work is constrained to 1080p.

- Short: `1080x1920`, 30 fps, normally 30–60 seconds.
- Longform build target: no more than 5 minutes by default.
- Longform hard build exception: no more than 10 minutes, and only for an explicit story/validation reason.
- No 4K during build or pre-soak.
- Historical 14-minute/4K experiments remain capability evidence only.

Current free narrator remains local Kokoro `af_heart`, starting from the accepted settings `speed=1.06`, `lang=en-us`.

ElevenLabs, premium voice cloning, AI avatar/presenter systems, and equivalent paid/premium generation are deferred until the correct core proof is accepted and the unattended production soak is stable.

## 2. Transcript-first V2 production

The canonical spoken transcript becomes a first-class hash-bound product artifact.

Target derivation chain:

`governed story/evidence -> analytical map -> canonical spoken transcript -> transcript truth/analysis/engagement QA -> pronunciation/voice intent -> visual/asset plan -> free-form Remotion -> proxy/actual-media review -> picture lock -> Kokoro/official-clip/music mix -> final audio -> final-audio-aligned transcript/captions -> transcript-derived SEO package -> platform-neutral package -> owner review`

Final captions, chapters, title/description/SEO packaging, and later localized scripts must derive from the actual accepted final-audio state, not from stale draft narration or unreviewed platform auto-caption output.

Transcript blocking errors include incorrect names, numbers, dates, currencies, units, negation, observation-vs-forecast status, speaker identity, official-clip boundaries, missing/duplicated sentences, materially corrupted transcript sections, or caption timing mismatch.

## 3. Editorial benchmark direction

The committed 30-video Bloomberg/Bloomberg Television/CNBC/Reuters/WSJ corpus is research/reference only and grants no reuse rights.

Adopt patterns rather than copy text:

- concrete tension before abstraction;
- visible event/object/person/location when available;
- one clear viewer promise;
- mechanism before generic commentary;
- hard evidence before inference;
- counterforce/uncertainty;
- clean watch/confirmation condition;
- title/opening/thumbnail/search alignment;
- sparse mechanism-aware deadpan wit.

Humor budget is descriptive, not a deterministic quota:

- Short: normally 0–1 useful line;
- <=5 minutes: normally 0–2;
- 5–10 minutes: normally 0–3.

No humor may create a claim, weaken uncertainty, imply trading advice, distort official speech, trivialize casualties/human hardship, or replace explanation.

## 4. Multilingual platform contract

The accepted multilingual/package substrate remains closed input during the current core proof and targeted repair.

Later activation model:

### YouTube longform

Reuse one approved picture master. Add creator-supplied language audio tracks, subtitles, translated titles/descriptions, and localized thumbnails where supported. Preserve creator-owned governed dub artifacts instead of relying on platform automatic dubbing as sole authority.

### YouTube Shorts

Multilingual audio exists as a product capability, but exact manual switching/default routing and client behavior must be validated on the target Capital Chronicle account before universal compatibility is claimed. Keep a localized-upload fallback.

### TikTok organic

Treat one ordinary post as one audio program. A localized dub is a separate remuxed media/post package unless future official product documentation proves a selectable multi-audio organic player.

Locale activation hardening—translation benchmark, language priority, voice selection, human listening, CJK/Indic/RTL typography QA, and exact account/client validation—starts only after the correct core proof and stable soak.

## 5. Music and official interview/speech policy

Grounded search may discover background music and official interview/speech candidates, but discoverability/scraping is not reuse authority.

Music-rights preference:

1. owned/commissioned;
2. explicit cross-platform licensed;
3. verified public-domain/CC0/permissive;
4. YouTube Audio Library for YouTube-scoped derivatives;
5. TikTok Commercial Music Library for TikTok-scoped derivatives;
6. no music.

Platform-library rights are not assumed cross-platform.

Official clips require speaker, institution, event/date, source, exact clip in/out, transcript, rights holder/restrictions, and allowed-use basis. Authentic speech must not be synthetically extended or silently redubbed to imply that the speaker spoke another language.
