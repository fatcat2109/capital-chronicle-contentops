# Samsung SDI reports 1 GWh US storage deal, Yonhap says

Yonhap reports a further agreement with Alpha Omega Power, with supply due by December. The latest 1 GWh is part of a 1.5 GWh two-agreement total.

Samsung SDI says its US subsidiary has agreed to provide Alpha Omega Power with 1 gigawatt-hour of energy-storage battery solutions, with supply due by December, Yonhap News Agency reported on 26 Oct 2026. The announcement describes a signed agreement; it does not establish that the batteries have been delivered.

## What the agreement covers

The latest deal covers 1 GWh, according to Yonhap's account of Samsung SDI's statement. Including the agreement signed last year brings the reported combined volume to 1.5 GWh. The latest agreement is already included in that total, so the two figures should not be added together.

According to Yonhap News Agency, the product is Samsung Battery Box and production is planned at StarPlus Energy in Indiana. That remains a stated manufacturing plan, rather than evidence of completed production or delivery.

## What the agreement leaves open

Yonhap also reports talks with other US customers. Those discussions are separate from the agreement with Alpha Omega Power; they should not be counted as additional signed orders.

A second signed agreement would be a more concrete commercial signal than a prospective sale. The counterweight is the economics: Yonhap gives only a broad value description for the combined agreements, without an exact price or profit estimate. Even if supply takes place as described, this announcement alone would not establish an earnings improvement or a wider storage-demand upswing.

The next useful test is whether supply happens by the reported December deadline. Evidence of delivery and clearer contract economics would help readers judge execution; more customer talks alone would not answer those questions.
