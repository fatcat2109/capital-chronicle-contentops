# Reuters reports gold below $4,000 on October 23

Reuters put the week's decline at 4% and linked gold's weakness to energy prices and US rate expectations. The spot quote was an intraday reading.

## Gold in Reuters’ October 23 report

In its [October 23, 2026 report carried by Kitco](https://www.kitco.com/news/off-the-wire/2026-10-23/gold-set-steep-weekly-loss-rising-oil-prices-bolster-fed-rate-hike), Reuters put spot gold at $3,998.35 per ounce at 15:20 UTC, down 0.6%. It described the first move below $4,000 since mid-July.

Reuters separately put the week's gold loss at 4%, leaving it heading for its weakest weekly performance since June. That was a week-to-date measure, not a completed weekly result.

## The explanation Reuters offered

Reuters linked gold's weakness to higher energy prices, inflation concerns and expectations of a US rate increase ahead of the following week's Federal Reserve meeting. That is Reuters' account of the pressure, not an independently established cause or a Fed decision.

The spot quote is an intraday observation. A later quote would be needed to show whether gold remained below $4,000; a final weekly result would require a closing reading.
