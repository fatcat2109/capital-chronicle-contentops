## 2. Product outcome

The Relay turns financially important first-party public speech/video into a clean, source-bound
Capital Chronicle social derivative quickly and with little operator burden:

```text
financially relevant source-video locator
-> exact original-source and event binding
-> practical rights triage
-> transcript/timing lock
-> LLM-assisted high-value segment ranking
-> deterministic quote/context/span validation
-> lightweight clip/reframe/captions
-> Capital Chronicle why-it-matters context
-> platform-neutral social-ready package on PUBLICATION_HOLD
-> existing publication owner only after exact authority permits
```

The source footage is evidence/source material. Capital Chronicle's selection, context, and
financial explanation are the product.

Representative inputs include central-bank speeches and press conferences, government/policy
speeches and hearings, market-moving senior-official remarks, and first-party company/IR/keynote/
earnings-event remarks from financially important executives.

## 3. Product doctrine and non-goals

Optimize for fast newsroom value, clear provenance, low implementation complexity, and repeatable
social output. Prefer the exact segment worth watching plus a concise explanation of why it matters.

This lane is not:

- a cinematic or fully authored V2 video;
- a general article-to-video factory;
- a generic viral-clipping or personality-fan account;
- another V1 editor, scheduler, durable store, publication coordinator, or truth owner;
- a legal-analysis or automated fair-use decision platform;
- a route for synthetic real-person footage, cloned speaker audio, or invented quotes;
- permission to scrape broadcaster/licensed feeds, defeat access controls, or use private sessions;
- publication authority.

Default production grammar is deliberately modest: authentic source speech, one context-safe
segment, restrained Capital Chronicle framing, source/speaker/date attribution, readable captions,
and a clean brand resolve. TTS narration, music, stock montage, generated imagery, longform chapters,
complex motion systems, multilingual output, and cinematic scene authorship are not first-release
requirements.

## 5. Source and rights doctrine

Public availability is discovery evidence only. A public web page, YouTube/X/TikTok post, speaker
identity, attribution, crop, subtitles, or short duration never proves reuse permission.

Every candidate receives exactly one automation triage state:

### `REUSE_CLEAR`

Eligible for zero-write production when exact evidence binds the original owner/source and an
applicable public-domain, open-license, owned, licensed, or express-reuse basis. Preserve attribution,
logo/seal restrictions, third-party exclusions, modification requirements, and any other exact term.
This state permits artifact construction only; public dispatch still requires Relay publication
authority.

### `TRANSFORMATIVE_EDITORIAL_REVIEW_REQUIRED`

The owner/source is known but no sufficiently clear reuse grant is established. A genuinely minimal
excerpt may be useful only as part of substantial Capital Chronicle selection, explanation,
criticism, or analysis. Automation may create a private review package if the source is lawfully
accessible and no technical restriction is bypassed, but it may not declare fair use, permission,
or public eligibility. Exact owner/legal/rights review is required before publication.

### `REJECT_NO_REUSE`

Reject when ownership is unknown, terms prohibit the use, the source is a third-party broadcaster or
licensed-feed/reupload, rights evidence is missing or contradictory, third-party speech/footage/
music/marks cannot be safely isolated, or access would require DRM/anti-bot/session circumvention.
Keep the reason and provenance locator; produce no clip.

Rights triage is a product boundary, not a legal conclusion. Ambiguity fails closed. A text-only
editorial opportunity may be referred to V1 through normal V1 ownership, but the Relay must not turn
a rejected clip into an accepted one.

## 6. Required provenance and source artifacts

Before selection, persist a source packet that binds at minimum:

- original owner/publisher, canonical source URL, platform media/event ID, event title/date, speaker
  identity/role, and observation time;
- source acquisition method, exact local media identity/hash where bytes are lawfully retained,
  duration, frame/timebase metadata, and immutable source locator when bytes cannot be retained;
- rights-policy URL, captured/snapshotted policy bytes or exact version, policy hash, checked-at time,
  rights triage state, attribution, and restrictions;
- transcript/caption source, transcript bytes/hash/version, timing basis, and confidence class;
- third-party questioner, interviewer, broadcaster, music, logo/seal, audience, or other embedded
  material assessment;
- event/report epistemic state and every public factual source used by Capital Chronicle context.

After selection, the clip packet additionally binds source in/out, context window, exact speaker,
exact quote and quote hash, omitted context note, transformation/crop/caption operations, output
media hash, and the editorial explanation/claim references.

## 7. Transcript and timing contract

Use explicit transcript authority classes:

1. `OFFICIAL_TIMED_TRANSCRIPT` — preferred;
2. `OFFICIAL_TRANSCRIPT_ALIGNED_TO_SOURCE`;
3. `OWNER_PUBLISHED_CAPTIONS`;
4. `ASR_DERIVED_REVIEW_REQUIRED` — discovery/segmentation only until exact audio verification.

An LLM or ASR transcript is not source truth by itself. Every public quote must be verified against
the bound source audio and, where available, official transcript/caption bytes. Speaker changes,
questions, interruptions, edits, applause, and missing context remain explicit. The first useful
release requires official timed text or owner-published timed captions; generalized ASR is optional
later work.

## 8. Highlight selection contract

The model receives only a bounded source packet, transcript/context window, relevant governed
newsroom context, and recent published-memory summary. It may propose/rank candidate spans but has no
factual, quote, rights, numeric, permission, or publication authority.

Rank for:

- new or materially clarifying information;
- policy signal, executive guidance, or market-relevant implication;
- specificity and financial/news value;
- a self-contained thought whose meaning survives the cut;
- authentic speaker/audio clarity;
- novelty against recent Capital Chronicle output;
- strength of the exact source and rights state.

Reject or abstain when a cut changes meaning, strips a material qualifier, merges separate answers,
attributes a question to the speaker, overstates an event/market consequence, repeats generic talking
points, contains inseparable third-party material, or lacks safe rights/provenance.

For every proposed highlight, return source in/out, exact quote, context-before/context-after summary,
why it matters financially, uncertainty/qualification, rights state, and rejection risks.
Deterministic validation chooses whether a frozen proposal may render. No useful safe highlight is a
valid `ABSTAIN_NO_SAFE_HIGHLIGHT` outcome.

Use the minimum sufficient model/provider. The contract is provider-agnostic and must not depend on
V2's heavyweight creative-worker policy. A weaker/cheaper model is acceptable only after it matches
the hard quote/context/selection requirements on bounded evaluation cases.

## 9. Lightweight production and expected artifacts

The first production shape is one source clip, normally roughly 15–60 seconds when the complete
thought naturally fits; duration never establishes rights and padding is forbidden.

Required outputs:

- one clean 1080x1920 H.264/AAC vertical master using authentic source speech/audio;
- one captioned review/social derivative plus SRT and WebVTT sidecars;
- source/speaker/role/date attribution and a visible Capital Chronicle identity;
- a concise headline, why-it-matters explanation, source credit, epistemic label, and canonical
  source link;
- source, rights, transcript, selection, clip/edit, media-probe, and package manifests with hashes;
- one platform-neutral publication package with `PUBLICATION_HOLD`, destination intents absent or
  explicitly undispatched, public writes `0`, and `UNKNOWN_WRITE=0`.

Reframing must keep the speaker and relevant source context visible, respect restricted marks/safe
zones, and avoid implying endorsement. Authentic source audio remains authentic; do not clone,
lip-sync, or replace the speaker. Once the source and highlight decision are frozen, clip extraction,
reframing, captions, and package materialization should be deterministic and locally reproducible.

## 12. First implementation milestone definition of done

The smallest useful release is complete when one real `REUSE_CLEAR` first-party speech source:

1. has exact original-source, speaker/event, media, transcript, policy, rights, restriction, and hash
   binding;
2. produces at least one model-proposed segment or truthfully abstains;
3. passes deterministic speaker/quote/span/context/third-party/rights validation;
4. builds one clean and one captioned 1080x1920 derivative plus SRT/WebVTT;
5. carries concise Capital Chronicle financial context that introduces no unsupported fact or
   proprietary numeric authority;
6. materializes hash-bound source/rights/transcript/selection/edit/package receipts;
7. is visually and audibly inspected as an actual rendered artifact;
8. performs zero public/provider/browser writes, creates no scheduler/store/publisher, does not
   mutate V1, and ends with `UNKNOWN_WRITE=0` and `PUBLICATION_HOLD`.
