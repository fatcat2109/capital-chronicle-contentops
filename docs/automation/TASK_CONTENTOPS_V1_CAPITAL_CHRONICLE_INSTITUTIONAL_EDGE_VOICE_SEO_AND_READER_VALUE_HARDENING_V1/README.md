## Thirty-piece comparative corpus

The research corpus intentionally spans wire reporting, market journalism, institutional analysis, general-reader business journalism, investor packaging, and a high-energy contrast case. The purpose is to learn editorial mechanics, never to copy prose.

### Reuters — factual spine and attribution discipline

1. China's Q2 Economic Growth Cools to 3-1/2-Year Low as Imbalances Worsen
2. China's Top Leaders Pledge Incremental Measures to Support Economy
3. Fed's Hammack Tells CNBC Rate Hikes May Be Needed to Quell High Inflation
4. Fed's Williams Says Monetary Policy 'Well Positioned' for 'Unusual' Circumstances
5. Fed's Williams Says Rates Are Well Positioned

Transferable mechanics:

- put the event and material magnitude first;
- keep attribution adjacent to the claim;
- add the relevant comparison early;
- use short paragraphs;
- expand from factual spine to structural implication only after the source record is secure;
- use human examples to concretize data rather than replace it.

Capital Chronicle use: breaking briefs, data releases, policy decisions, first paragraphs of all article modes.

### Bloomberg — mechanism compression and cross-asset causality

6. Asia Heat Waves Spell Double Trouble for Economies Hit by Oil
7. Oil Shock Is Adding Stress to the Global Economy
8. AI Boom Drowns Out War Fears to Fuel Asia's Great Market Divide
9. Markets Are Right to Worry About Growth
10. Dollar Enjoys Petrocurrency Status as Oil Drives Markets

Transferable mechanics:

- compress a market thesis into the headline and first paragraphs;
- identify the force dominating the current reaction function;
- move from macro driver to sectors/assets without losing the thesis;
- use controlled metaphor sparingly;
- explain why the market's prior framework changed.

Capital Chronicle use: mechanism-led analysis, cross-asset explanation, rate/FX/commodity transmission.

### Wall Street Journal — tension-led explanation

11. China's Economy Weakens on Several Fronts as Property Bust Worsens
12. Inflation Was 3.4% in July, Down Slightly From the Previous Month
13. The Jobless Boom Has Arrived
14. Economists Shrug Off Trumponomics, Boost 2026 Growth Outlook Back Above 2%

Transferable mechanics:

- build the article around a genuine contradiction or tension;
- use data to test the premise rather than decorate it;
- explain what would make the thesis fail;
- connect the macro story to companies, workers, households, or investors.

Capital Chronicle use: regime shifts, productivity/employment, inflation/growth, fiscal stimulus versus bond discipline, good-news/bad-market-reaction stories.

### Financial Times — institutional depth and structural reasoning

15. Broken FIMA
16. Britain's Debt Cage
17. China Investment Slump Deepens as Economy Shows Signs of Weakness
18. Private Credit's Woes Continue

Transferable mechanics:

- follow institutional incentives and balance sheets;
- explain governance and market plumbing when they change the interpretation;
- introduce historical context only when it is analytically useful;
- assume an intelligent reader but define specialist concepts at first use;
- use understatement instead of performative cleverness.

Capital Chronicle use: deep policy, liquidity, sovereign, fiscal, credit, and institutional analysis.

### New York Times — accessibility without intellectual dilution

19. Jittery Markets Seek an Iran 'Off-Ramp'
20. Code Red for India's Coders
21. A $3.2 Trillion Deal-Making Frenzy Is Spurred by the A.I. Economy
22. Amazon to Cut 16,000 Jobs in Latest Round of Layoffs

Transferable mechanics:

- connect structural change to people, firms, and concrete decisions;
- define specialist concepts in plain language;
- expand from a human-scale example to the larger economic system;
- orient readers with compact explanatory sections and useful questions.

Capital Chronicle use: explainers, technology/labor stories, cold-reader orientation.

### ZeroHedge — contrast case for kinetic energy

23. AI Demand Meets $100 Oil
24. The Maddening 'Ground Hog Day' Markets Pt 1
25. It's Not About Oil: The Global Order Is Quietly Unraveling
26. The World's Hottest Trade Is Built on Fake Money—and It's About to Collapse

Useful mechanics:

- fast tempo;
- memorable thesis;
- strong rhetorical pivots;
- compact summary packaging;
- willingness to connect a market move to a broader mechanism.

Explicitly rejected:

- unsupported certainty;
- sensational/apocalyptic framing;
- ideology presented as analysis;
- all-caps urgency;
- direct trading promotion;
- cheap dunking;
- hardship jokes;
- claims stronger than the source record.

Capital Chronicle should borrow only the kinetic energy, not the epistemic looseness.

### Barron's — investor utility and packaging

Capital Chronicle use: investor-oriented explanation without buy/sell/hold recommendations.

## Synthesis

The editorial priority stack is deliberately asymmetric:

1. factual and attribution discipline;
2. mechanism and causal structure;
3. institutional depth;
4. tension and explanatory narrative;
5. human accessibility;
6. investor utility;
7. restrained personality.

Personality is last because it may improve retention but must never outrank truth.

## Capital Chronicle differentiator

The product should not compete by being louder. It should compete by making uncertainty legible and mechanisms useful.

A strong Capital Chronicle article should leave the reader with:

- the most important fact;
- the mechanism connecting it to the economy/market;
- the countervailing force;
- the boundary between source fact and Capital Chronicle analysis;
- the observable checkpoint that can confirm or challenge the view.

That makes the product more useful than generic recap and more trustworthy than conviction theater.

## SEO research decision

SEO is a packaging layer, not a truth layer.

The durable policy is people-first and evidence-governed:

- canonical editorial headline for brand/readability;
- descriptive search title for query clarity;
- native social hook for distribution;
- all three bound to the same supported proposition;
- useful first-screen answer rather than delayed keyword setup;
- stable concise slug;
- accurate meta description;
- structured Article/NewsArticle metadata only when supported by the destination;
- descriptive internal links when useful;
- no keyword stuffing, fixed word-count target, mandatory FAQ, or search-engine-first filler.

Search-specific learning remains `HOLD` until search-specific evidence exists. Generic social engagement cannot mutate SEO policy.

## Gen-Z / high-interaction decision
