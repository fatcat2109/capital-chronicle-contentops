The Policy Corridor Holds as the Central Bank Waits

The official notice preserves the current position while leaving the next decision dependent on new evidence.

By Capital Chronicle

The central bank notice confirmed that the policy corridor remains unchanged. The record preserves the current position rather than announcing a new course, which makes the next decision a question of incoming evidence rather than fresh policy action.

What the notice changes—and what it does not

The useful distinction is between a position that has not changed and an outlook that still can. The notice establishes the first point. It does not settle how officials will respond when the evidence changes.

That restraint matters because a steady setting can coexist with a changing reaction function. In plain English: the instrument is still, but the conditions governing its next move are not.

The next observable checkpoint

The next dated official notice is the clean checkpoint. It would show whether the current position remains intact, whether the central bank changes its description of the evidence, or whether a new decision has become warranted.
