## Owner visual findings elevated to product doctrine

Recent owner actual-media review established:

1. Overall visual quality, color, and transitions can already reach a strong professional direction.
2. Repetition of the same asset or visually equivalent asset family remains a material defect.
3. Chart images, native charts, primary-data visuals, and document evidence should usually remain legible as complete analytical objects.
4. Ornamental zoom-in, zoom-out, pan, or parallax on charts is forbidden.
5. Chart motion is justified only when it communicates information: progressive reveal, series comparison, annotation, inflection emphasis, or narration-linked analytical focus.
6. Diversity must be measured from actual rendered dependencies, visual families, semantic purposes, consecutive use, recent-use history, and cumulative screen time—not merely asset IDs.
7. Rights-safe is necessary but not sufficient. Weak professional-presentation assets should be rejected.
8. Concrete/contextual media, primary documents, native charts/maps, and evidence treatments precede abstract geometry.
9. Real-person documentary imagery must be real and rights-cleared. Generated media is never factual/documentary authority.
