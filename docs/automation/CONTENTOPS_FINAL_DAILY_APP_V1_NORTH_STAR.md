## Current quality-probation operating overlay

Jim's current pragmatic V1 policy is `QUALITY_PROBATION_FOUR_WINDOW_V1`. The canonical editorial
brain is a fresh native Codex Desktop `gpt-5.6-sol / XHIGH` task at exactly four routine Bangkok
opportunities: 17:00, 21:00, and 23:00 Monday-Friday plus 01:00 Tuesday-Saturday. There is no
publication minimum, no filler permission, and no automatic schedule shift or scale-up. Material
events prioritize the next scheduled opportunity; manual `GO` is the sole explicit extra
opportunity and bypasses no gate.

A qualified opportunity is one complete transaction: recover safe prior work and due observations;
evaluate/load the active policy; produce and gate at most one article; confirm canonical Substack;
attempt every currently READY V1 derivative once; reconcile; and schedule observations. Immutable
bounded learning covers content/editorial, SEO, destination/package, and timing recommendations.
Timing is observation/recommendation only while schedule ownership remains locked to Jim. The next
fresh XHIGH task consumes that policy as preference-only context with zero truth, numeric,
permission, gate, or publication authority.

The older 24/7 supervision language remains long-term architecture direction, not evidence that a
separate always-on expensive model worker is required or currently proven. The four native tasks
plus canonical durable control plane are the owner-approved quality-probation mode.

## 1. Owner product decision

ContentOps V1 must become one complete autonomous daily application that Jim can leave running 24/7.

The product is not a collection of scripts, a manual publishing workbench, a once-per-day cron, or a newsroom demo. It is an always-on autonomous newsroom, distribution system, publication manager, performance observer, and bounded learning loop.

The final product should require Jim to manage policy and exceptional incidents, not manually drive routine content production.

The north-star experience is:

```text
start ContentOps Daily App
→ leave it running continuously
→ it maintains intake, health, readback, metrics, schedule, and recovery while idle
→ at the best currently-authorized editorial windows, or on a genuinely material event, it wakes the expensive newsroom path
→ selects or abstains
→ acquires exact evidence
→ writes / reviews / optimizes / visualizes
→ packages natively for every currently READY destination
→ publishes automatically when all exact gates pass
→ reads back and reconciles public objects
→ observes engagement / search / subscriber outcomes over time
→ evaluates what worked and what did not
→ makes bounded evidence-backed improvements to timing, SEO, editorial packaging, and platform strategy
→ continues indefinitely
```

No-publication remains a valid outcome. The application must never manufacture filler merely because a scheduled window occurred.

Engagement can change editorial priority and packaging policy. It can never change factual truth, source authority, evidence classification, permissions, Capital Chronicle analysis, or numeric truth.

## 4. Always-on means low-cost supervision, not continuous expensive LLM work

The app may run 24/7, but expensive newsroom/model work must only wake when justified.

Idle responsibilities should be deterministic or cheap wherever possible:

- process health and heartbeat;
- durable-state reconciliation;
- X/headline sidecar availability and freshness checks;
- scheduled-window calculation;
- material-event trigger evaluation from governed discovery metadata;
- destination health/readiness refresh where safe;
- public-object readback/reconciliation when required;
- due performance observations;
- due learning-policy evaluation;
- incident state and kill-switch observation.

Expensive semantic/model work begins only for a due editorial decision or a justified material-event trigger.

Do not call LLMs continuously just because the process is alive.

## 5. Editorial cadence becomes learned policy

The historical launch cadence of one core decision per day is a safe bootstrap, not the final scheduling product.

The final app must maintain a versioned `EditorialWindowPolicy` (exact naming is implementation judgment) containing, at minimum:

- timezone / market-session context;
- eligible core newsroom windows;
- per-platform publication-delay or preferred-window policy where useful;
- material-event wakeup rules;
- minimum spacing / saturation controls;
- freshness limits;
- exploration allowance;
- confidence / sample-size state;
- policy version and effective time.

At bootstrap, use deterministic configured defaults. Do not claim that unmeasured times are optimal.

After a future explicit Jim owner decision unlocks schedule mutation, real outcomes may support
shifting windows toward stronger qualified engagement while preserving:

- news freshness;
- evidence availability;
- platform constraints;
- update-chain urgency;
- anti-spam spacing;
- portfolio diversity;
- no-publication freedom.

A high-engagement historical window cannot justify delaying breaking material news until it becomes stale.

## 6. Material-event wakeups

The always-on app may execute outside normal editorial windows when a genuinely material new event or update warrants it.

Use the existing governed headline/update-chain system and deterministic materiality metadata. Do not create a second breaking-news engine.

During current quality probation, material-event metadata prioritizes the next scheduled
opportunity and does not create a fifth automatic XHIGH run. If a future owner decision restores
extra material-event wakes, they must still pass the same evidence, article-mode, review,
publication, and readback gates as a scheduled cycle.

## 7. Evidence and authority remain hard gates

Evidence authority remains:

```text
story type + article mode → exact evidence profile
```

ContentOps factual reporting may use exact authorized primary-source evidence.

Capital Chronicle authority remains mandatory for analytical, scenario, forecast, valuation, market-impact, macro, probabilistic, regime, or other analytical/numeric claims when the resolved evidence contract requires it.

X/social content is discovery and priority input only. It is never factual or numeric evidence.

Evidence reachability may inform ranking but grants no truth or publication authority.

The application must never learn to weaken evidence requirements merely because weaker stories historically received more engagement.

## 8. Canonical article and media production

The canonical rolling-X grounded article/media builder is the Tier-1 article/media subsystem.

It must consume only accepted story/evidence/authority state and produce downstream-compatible article, SEO metadata, source-backed media, and exact immutable bindings.

Current known safety issues at the 2026-08-09 baseline must be corrected before V1 release:

1. framing/X-derived `entities_topics` must never be mislabeled as facts copied from accepted evidence;
2. deterministic Capital Chronicle rendering rights must be separated from the rights/status of underlying source content; do not overclaim `capital_chronicle_owned` for official excerpts or source material;
3. official-policy acquisition must use a working bounded first-party Federal Reserve/FOMC discovery path rather than the currently observed 404 route;
4. unsupported source rights or insufficient source-backed visual diversity must fail closed.

Do not lower the existing article/media gate to avoid these corrections.

## 10. Post lifecycle management

A public object is not complete at `send`.

The Daily App must track each published identity through:

```text
intent
→ exact payload
→ dispatch
→ known object / unknown write
→ strict readback
→ reconciliation if needed
→ observation schedule
→ performance snapshots
→ retrospective / learning eligibility
```

Every public object must retain story, article, platform variant, destination, publication window, payload hash, object ID/URL hash, readback state, and performance-observation identity.

Unknown writes remain:

`STOP RETRY → READ BACK → RECONCILE`

Never blind retry.

## 11. Real performance observation

The final app must replace manual-only performance replay as the primary production path.

Collect the metrics actually exposed by each authorized platform through an existing safe API/non-browser binding where available, or through the canonical publishing/readback browser profile when explicitly supported and safe.

Do not inspect/export cookies, browser storage, tokens, credentials, or session databases.

Store platform-native metrics without pretending they are directly comparable.

Useful observations include, where the platform exposes them:

- impressions / reach;
- meaningful reads / completion;
- likes / reactions;
- replies / comments;
- reposts / shares;
- saves / bookmarks;
- canonical-link clicks;
- profile visits;
- follower/subscriber delta;
- newsletter opens / clicks;
- subscriber conversion;
- search impressions / clicks / CTR / queries / position;
- return readership / landing engagement where first-party analytics exists.

Missing metrics remain unavailable, never zero.

Observation schedules should be platform-aware and bounded. The system may use checkpoints such as early, same-day, next-day, and longer-tail observations, but exact timings should be configuration/policy rather than hardcoded product truth.

## 12. Closed-loop learning

Learning optimizes qualified engagement and product utility, not vanity metrics alone.

The objective function should reward meaningful reads, completion, shares, saves, qualified replies, canonical clicks, subscriber conversion, search longevity, repeat readership, and efficient accepted-package production.

Penalize clickbait, repetition, concentration, weak evidence, overclaim, short-lived outrage, high revision burden, high cost, and low-delta updates.

Learning may update only bounded editorial/product policy such as:

- editorial decision windows;
- platform-specific publication windows or delay strategy;
- story-priority weights;
- headline style / lede / framing choices;
- SEO intent / keyword cluster / structure / refresh strategy;
- content mode and depth;
- visual/package choices;
- destination-specific package form;
- portfolio concentration penalties.

Learning must never mutate:

- source authority;
- evidence facts;
- permissions;
- numeric truth;
- Capital Chronicle analytical output;
- safety gates;
- account identity;
- public-write authority.

Use minimum sample sizes, confidence classes, policy versioning, bounded step sizes, rollback, and exploration/holdout where useful. Small samples create observations, not major automatic policy changes.

## 13. SEO is both production and measured learning

Every canonical long-form article should record:

- primary search intent;
- secondary intents;
- target reader;
- query/keyword cluster;
- news-versus-evergreen balance;
- SEO title / reader headline;
- slug / meta description;
- section structure;
- primary-source links;
- update strategy;
- canonical URL.

Deterministic SEO audit scores are quality checks, not observed success.

Observed search metrics, when connected, may improve future intent selection, headline/structure, internal-linking, timing, and refresh decisions. They may not distort factual framing or certainty.

## 16. Operating modes

The final app exposes the existing exact modes:

- `AUTONOMOUS_DEFAULT` — product default; full routine automation including public writes only when exact deterministic gates pass;
- `SUPERVISED_OPERATOR_GATE` — optional owner pause before public write;
- `SHADOW_ONLY` — full workflow with zero public writes;
- `KILL_SWITCH` — block new public writes while preserving readback, reconciliation, metrics, and recovery.

The kill switch must be observable by the always-on controller before every new public write.
