## 1. Answer-to-calculation accountability

Attach a compact receipt to the existing question/run/report with:
- Exact question/scope/cutoff, source edition, instrument/proxy and horizon.
- Producer/module/model version, input and output identities, validation identity and clocks.
- NEW_INVOCATION, REUSED_VALID_OUTPUT, UNAVAILABLE_INPUT, NOT_APPLICABLE or NOT_VERIFIED.
- Separate invocation outcome, output validity and consumer usage/reference. A function can
  run and return missing; a valid output can be unused. A reused output is not a new call.
- Which actual answer paragraph, claim or deterministic decision input cites each result;
  a citation establishes a recorded dependency, not empirical usefulness or causal effect.
- Cache dependency keys and invalidation rules. No rerun merely to make a log look current.

A combined list of consumer names, a counter or green test cannot substitute for this join.
Unknown historical invocation/usage stays unknown. Instrument at the owner seam first;
do not require invasive logging changes to every layer before useful product delivery.

## 2. Probability is always probability of a defined event

An applicable probability contract preserves:
- target/event definition and any threshold/transform, outcome resolution rule;
- instrument and analytical proxy/basis, units/currency where relevant;
- forecast origin/knowledge cutoff, horizon/session/calendar and outcome availability clock;
- probability measure (physical/statistical, subjective challenger or risk-neutral),
  conditioning/selection population and model/method version;
- provenance of inputs/parameters and evaluation state with its exact cohort/period.

Terminal direction, barrier-first passage, net-after-cost profit and macro scenario occurrence
are different events. Probability from one cannot be copied to another, even if both are 0–1.
Risk-neutral option valuation probabilities are not physical profit probabilities.
A score or an LLM's verbal confidence is not a calibrated win probability.

Preserve existing explicitly scoped, frozen, prospectively evaluated LLM structured numeric
challenger contracts. This clarification does not ban those existing research roles. An
LLM challenger estimate must remain labelled by source/method and evaluation state; it
cannot overwrite observed truth, Task27's numeric priors/posteriors/weights, risk accounting,
canonical scoring or execution authority. The ordinary qualitative owner path need not
invent a posterior or estimate unsupported probabilities to complete an answer.

Normalize a categorical scenario set only when its events are mutually exclusive and
exhaustive within the explicit model scope. Macro mechanisms that can coexist are not
such a partition. Unmodeled possibilities remain a limitation; no invented residual number.
Zero sample count does not prove impossibility. Structural 0/1 probabilities need explicit
support; no automatic smoothing/pseudocount is adopted by this doctrine.

## 3. Selection and dependent evidence

Keep the observation channel distinct from the economic event: scheduled release, SEC filing,
selected X list/filter, retained dossier or owner-selected holdings. Preserve known funnel IDs
and reasons; unknown mechanisms stay unknown. No feed item is not evidence of no event unless
the observation process justifies that inference. A feed/filter change alone is not a change
in economic probability. Avoid applying the same selection adjustment twice.

Task27 already rejects duplicate evidence IDs/underlying hashes. Additional grouping must
distinguish syndicated copies, indicators derived from one price series, shared release
components, actual new components and revisions. A shared event alone does not make every
component a duplicate; a new URL alone does not make information independent.
Use justified joint/conditional likelihoods when already available. Otherwise choose a
defensible representative for numeric use or label sensitivity/unknown dependence; do not
multiply every headline likelihood or fabricate a correlation discount.
Primary/Critical agreement on the same model/evidence is not independent empirical confirmation.

Prior/likelihood provenance must state what was estimated, over which pre-cutoff sample,
under what target/horizon/regime and assumptions, and what remains unverified. A caller-supplied
hash or valid sum does not establish statistical validity. Effective sample size/uncertainty
must be measured by an applicable owner or remain unknown, never guessed from row count.

## 4. Forecast, decision and reasoning are different evaluation axes

Reuse existing shared quant ledger, calibration and model factory/trial owners.
Brier/log score require the same defined event/outcome; pinball requires compatible quantiles;
CRPS requires a compatible predictive distribution. Existing calibration's actual-positive
event must not silently become a generic calibrator for arbitrary barrier/net-profit/macro
targets. Model output state CALIBRATED describes that exact algorithm/window, not alpha.

Compare raw versus adjusted forecasts and baselines on matched targets, origins, horizons,
selection populations and common resolved rows, with forecast/outcome clocks frozen correctly.
Overlapping 5/21/63-session rows need dependence-aware uncertainty; more rows are not necessarily
more independent events. Reuse applicable chronological/block/purge/embargo/trial/DSR machinery,
only when overlap/data and design justify it. No arbitrary calendar duration or trade count
guarantees proof. Retain unresolved/no-trade cases and trial history, not winners only.

Build closure and evidence maturity are separate. A finite tested integration can close before
future outcomes arrive; no new scheduler activation, backfilled prospective credit or indefinite
WAIT is authorized. Forecast quality, after-cost decision value and reasoning/source quality
are reported independently.

## 5. Actions and payoff start from the same current state

Reuse existing payoff/cost/FOSD/covariance/stress owners. A high win rate is insufficient without
gain/loss sizes, costs, path/horizon and portfolio interactions. Terminal-price probability
cannot determine stop-before-target or realized financing/slippage without suitable inputs.

The offline challenger's NO_TRADE zero payoff is scoped to not opening a new exposure.
For existing holdings, doing nothing means retaining exposure and risk. Hold/reduce/close/switch
comparisons must share the same current wealth/exposure reference, remaining payoff and transition
costs; do not erase holding risk or count sunk PnL twice. This doctrine does not silently expand
the offline challenger or choose new canonical preferences/weights/Kelly sizing.
Empirical FOSD is a partial order on the supplied distribution, not out-of-sample dominance;
incomparable alternatives remain incomparable/abstain unless existing authorized preferences
and admissible inputs determine a choice.

Governed proxies may support analysis at compatible horizons. Execution/sizing/PnL claims still
require the actual contract and applicable authority; no exact live CFD quote is required
merely to explain an analysis-only thesis.
