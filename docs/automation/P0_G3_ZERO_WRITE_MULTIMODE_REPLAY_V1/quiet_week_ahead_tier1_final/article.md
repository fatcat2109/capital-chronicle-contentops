The BEA schedule therefore establishes two distinct August 26 releases at 8:30 AM: GDP (Second Estimate) and Corporate Profits, 2nd Quarter 2026, and Personal Income and Outlays, July 2026. ## What the calendar establishes

The GDP and corporate profits release covers the 2nd Quarter of 2026. The personal income and outlays release covers July 2026. Both are scheduled for August 26 at 8:30 AM. Nothing in the schedule establishes what the second estimate will show, what corporate profits will be, or what the July release will report.

## What to watch when the releases arrive

Keep the two scopes separate. One release concerns second-quarter GDP and corporate profits; the other concerns July personal income and outlays. Use the eventual releases, rather than the calendar, to establish their values and direction. Any wider conclusion must wait for evidence contained in those releases.

Until then, the boundary is straightforward: the date, time, release names and covered periods are scheduled facts. Every value and outcome remains unknown.
