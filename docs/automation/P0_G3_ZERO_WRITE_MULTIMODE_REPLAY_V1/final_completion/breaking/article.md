# Federal Reserve releases minutes from July FOMC meeting

_The official record covers the Federal Open Market Committee meeting held on July 28–29, 2026._

The Federal Reserve, via www.federalreserve.gov, released the minutes of the Federal Open Market Committee meeting held on July 28–29, 2026. The official release was published Wednesday and identifies the record as the minutes for that meeting. [www.federalreserve.gov](https://www.federalreserve.gov/newsevents/pressreleases/monetary20260819a.htm)

The release confirms that the committee’s meeting record is now available. It does not, within the accepted evidence for this brief, support claims about the minutes’ economic assessments, policy discussions, or any later policy decision. Those subjects therefore remain outside this update.

For readers tracking the Federal Reserve, the useful development is narrow but clear: the official minutes tied to the July meeting have been released. The document establishes the meeting dates and the release event; further conclusions would require evidence from the minutes themselves rather than inference from the announcement.
