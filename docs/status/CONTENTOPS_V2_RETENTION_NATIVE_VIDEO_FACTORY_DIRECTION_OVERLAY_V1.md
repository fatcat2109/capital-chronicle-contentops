## Current model/cost direction

ContentOps remains the product. Codex is the repository builder only.

V2 must amortize the existing newsroom/research/evidence/memory layer across content objects. Actual V1 production evidence shows newsroom/routing can exceed one million tokens before article/video work; therefore V2 should not repeatedly ask a frontier coding agent to rediscover and research the entire world for each video.

Use strong models at bounded high-value decisions, cheap capable models where measured quality is sufficient, and deterministic/local rendering/QA/caching where practical. Frontier models are escalation, not permanent control-loop owners.

Do not hardcode temporal provider pricing or subscription quotas into product architecture.
