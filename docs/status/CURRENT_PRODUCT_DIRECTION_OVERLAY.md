The canonical execution framework is `MAIN_CODEX` only. Fresh owner-authorized XHIGH editorial/creative workers may operate only at bounded lane-defined creative boundaries; approved 9Router models remain bounded low-cost non-authority helpers.

Capital Chronicle/Core Analyzer owns proprietary analytical/numeric/decision truth. ContentOps may not invent or alter missing CC numbers, calculations, forecasts, probabilities, scenarios, regimes, decision/watch/abstain outputs, paper records, or analytical error attribution.

Internal governance is not public permission. Public use of proprietary CC/Core Analyzer material requires an exact story-scoped publication-safe upstream artifact whose consumer/use, permissions, binding, lineage, freshness/source health, and blockers permit the intended ContentOps use. Arbitrary database rows and internally validated Analyzer handoffs remain context/private unless that separate publication contract exists.
