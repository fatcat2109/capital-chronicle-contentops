Newsroom brief: The Fed held on October 28. Both dissenters wanted a hike.
The Fed's October 28 hold was a 10-2 decision. Both dissenters wanted higher rates.
Context: The strongest countercase to a looming-hike interpretation is the decision itself: ten members approved
keeping the range unchanged. The two dissenters' preference is evidence of disagreement at this meeting, not a
forecast of the next vote. Reading the split as a timetable for higher rates would give it a meaning the vote alone
cannot establish.
