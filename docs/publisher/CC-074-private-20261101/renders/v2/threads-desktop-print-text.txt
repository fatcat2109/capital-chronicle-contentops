The Fed held on October 28. Both dissenters wanted a hike.
The strongest countercase to a looming-hike interpretation is the decision itself: ten members approved keeping the
range unchanged. The two dissenters' preference is evidence of disagreement at this meeting, not a forecast of the
next vote. Reading the split as a timetable for higher rates would give it a meaning the vote alone cannot establish.
