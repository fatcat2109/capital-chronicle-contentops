# Payrolls: the scenario map before the release

*Illustrative fixture only; these are fictional figures, not a market report.*

## Before the release

Payrolls is due Friday at 08:30 EDT. The test is whether labour demand is cooling broadly or merely normalising.

## Scenarios

### Cooling demand

Probability: 50%

Slower hiring and weaker wage pressure would favour a softer rates interpretation.

### Steady expansion

Probability: 30%

Hiring near the prior-pricing assumption would leave the broader policy debate unresolved.

### Renewed heat

Probability: 20%

Broad hiring and wage strength would challenge the cooling narrative.

## What is priced

The illustrative prior-pricing assumption is 160000 jobs; it is not an observed consensus.

## Levels of interest

The derived yield reference of 4.2% is a level to observe, not a transaction instruction.

## What would invalidate the view

The cooling interpretation would fail if payroll strength were broad and wage pressure accelerated. First-print revisions remain a separate uncertainty.
