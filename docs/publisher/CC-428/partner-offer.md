# Proposed pilot: Capital Chronicle release analysis

**For the partner selected by Ops Product. Draft terms; no agreement or delivery has occurred.**

Capital Chronicle proposes a four-piece, 30-day editorial pilot explaining how
major US releases affect gold, Bitcoin, US equities, EUR/USD, USD/JPY and WTI.
Each piece gives one clear argument, its main countercase and the observation
that would change our view. We offer original analysis with visible brand credit.

**Deliverable and selection.** Four briefs of at most 350 words, each with one
original, source-bound chart and its mandatory attribution. The editor chooses
the event with us. No filler, live trade service, exclusive scoop or performance
claim is promised. The proposed cadence changes no existing schedule.

**Credit and rights.** Credit each piece to **Capital Chronicle**. We retain
ownership and propose a non-exclusive licence for the selected partner's named
newsletter/community edition and its accessible archive. No onward syndication,
sale, sublicensing, AI training or unrelated reuse. Link only to a confirmed
Capital Chronicle Substack article, or carry no link. Keep required source,
chart and image credits. Supply our prose, computed outputs and cleared visuals;
never supply raw restricted datasets, subscriber data or private account details.

**Editorial and AI terms.** Our workflow uses AI-assisted drafting and editing.
Numbers must remain bound to Main's desk output or cited sources. Capital
Chronicle approves factual meaning before delivery; the partner owns its final
editorial selection. Agree the visible AI disclosure in writing. Edits that alter
numbers, attribution, uncertainty or analytical meaning return for our approval.

**Delivery and corrections.** Name the receiving editor, approved email/API
route, format and rate in the agreement. No browser automation or account access
is implied. We propose notifying the editor promptly of material corrections;
agree a 24-hour correction/takedown response and keep exact private source/version
records. A platform restriction stops that route. Never imply a piece was corrected
or removed before readback confirms it.

**Scope and notices.** This is general market analysis. Every piece carries:
“Capital Chronicle offers analysis only, not financial advice or recommendations.”
Conditional directional calls also retain their condition, horizon, invalidation,
scorecard binding and the applicable conflicts notice. No personalised advice,
account details, order instructions or guaranteed returns.

**Commercial and identity terms.** Proposed pilot fee: zero. No advertising
purchase, auto-renewal, subscription purchase or revenue guarantee. Public credit
uses our brand; Jim supplies any required legal identity privately during
contracting and signs the agreement himself. The counterparty must expressly
accept that arrangement. Either party may stop future deliveries; agree the
treatment of already-issued editions and corrections before the first piece.

**Review at the end.** Compare completed pieces, aggregate reads/clicks and useful
reader questions that the partner can lawfully share. Do not exchange personal
reader data. Any paid continuation needs a new agreement.
