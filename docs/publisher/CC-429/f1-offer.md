# Verified Calls: a record your readers can inspect

We help finance writers show what they said before the result was known. Our proposed pilot keeps your dated analysis in Capital Chronicle's existing calls-ledger format and gives you a linked scorecard badge.

**The pilot:** five writers, one month, no charge. You send each new eligible call through the attached intake form before its measurement window starts. We record its condition, direction, reference, horizon and invalidation. Changes and corrections become new records. Earlier versions remain visible.

**What your readers get:** a badge showing the number of resolved calls, the unresolved count, the measurement period and a link to the complete record. For example, before any results: “Verified Calls · 0 resolved · pilot record”. That is a design example, not an awarded badge. Verification covers the recorded statement and its timing; it does not certify skill, returns, identity or regulatory status.

**What we measure:** whether a predeclared outcome occurred, using the existing scorer and the same outcome contract for you and the named baseline. Missing observations remain unresolved. No cherry-picking of losses, late submissions or withdrawn calls. Probability calibration appears only when you supplied the corresponding probability before the outcome.

**What you provide:** your original public link, permission to display your own submitted words and badge, and the fields in the intake template. A long-term essay can contribute a precisely dated forecast; it need not become a short-term trading call. Content you cannot license stays linked rather than copied.

**What this is for:** analysis and accountability. It is not a signal service, model portfolio, copied-trading product or promise of investment performance. Readers make their own decisions. Capital Chronicle offers analysis only, not financial advice or recommendations.

**The price question:** at the end, would you continue at a proposed $19 per month? This is a willingness-to-pay question. There is no subscription, checkout, card collection or automatic renewal in the pilot.

**The smallest start:** Jim sends the prepared invitation to David Keller through Market Misbehavior's public contact form. If he opts in, Publisher handles the manual intake using a separate creator namespace in the existing record format. A creator's views stay attributed to that creator; they do not become the desk's analysis.

We would continue the test if five of the 20 prospects opt in, three submit eligible records in at least three weeks, and two say they would pay $19. These are proposed decision thresholds, not observed demand. If fewer respond, we learn from the replies before building billing or a new platform.
