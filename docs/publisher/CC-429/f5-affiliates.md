# Three charting affiliates: eligibility before a revenue test

Public pages checked on 11 November 2026. “Anonymous publisher” needs two answers: a public pen name or brand, and the private identity required by the programme and payer. None of these programmes establishes a right to hide the payee's identity. No application, account, terms acceptance, payout setup or link creation was performed.

| Programme | Public brand and Vietnam | Payout | Choice for the cheapest test |
|---|---|---|---|
| TradingView | Its named exclusions are Iran, North Korea, Syria, Cuba, and Crimea, Donetsk and Luhansk. Vietnam is not on that list. Sanctions, lawful service/payment availability and verification still apply. The reviewed rules do not expressly promise public pseudonymity. | PayPal only, USD, monthly. The rules aim to process payouts within 40 days. Account/traffic verification, requested tax documents and potentially identity proof precede payment. The minimum threshold can be set by TradingView; no fixed threshold is stated in the reviewed rules. | Strongest fit with our existing chart use and the clearest published geography rule. Country eligibility is an inference from that rule, not an approved Vietnamese affiliate account. |
| TrendSpider | The public programme expressly permits a public pseudonym and requires accurate private contact details. Its affiliate terms and linked programme/general rules did not expose an accepted-country or excluded-country list. Vietnam acceptance is therefore **not confirmed**. | Official help lists PayPal, domestic wire and international wire. It describes monthly payments on the following month's 15th, a 30-day clearance period and an unpaid balance exceeding $100. Contract terms allow verification holds up to 60 days. | **First inquiry:** it answers the public-pseudonym requirement directly. Ask its public affiliate contact to confirm Vietnam and the payout route before any application. This is an eligibility experiment, not a verified revenue route. |
| GoCharting | Its affiliate rules require accurate application/contact information; general terms require a legal full name. No programme country list or express public-pseudonym guarantee was found on the reviewed programme, affiliate terms and general terms pages. Vietnam acceptance is **not confirmed**. | Programme page says monthly bank wire, minimum approximately $100 or INR 5,000. Affiliate terms describe monthly customer commissions starting in the second month, annual-customer commissions on a one-time basis, and verification holds up to 60 days. The programme's lifetime marketing claim is not a promise of identical payment timing for annual and monthly plans. | Reserve candidate. Require an answer on Vietnam, the public brand, beneficiary requirements and international wire costs; do not assume an Indian payout threshold applies to a Vietnamese bank. |

Sources: [TradingView rules](https://www.tradingview.com/partner-rules/) (updated 30 September; effective 30 October 2026), [programme](https://www.tradingview.com/partner-program/); [TrendSpider programme](https://trendspider.com/affiliates/), [affiliate contract](https://trendspider.com/affiliates/terms-of-service/), [payout help](https://help.trendspider.com/kb/account-management/how-to-use-trendspider-affiliate-manager-account), [general terms](https://trendspider.com/terms-of-service/); [GoCharting programme](https://gocharting.com/partnerships/affiliate), [affiliate terms](https://gocharting.com/legal/affiliate), [general terms](https://gocharting.com/legal/terms).

TradingView and GoCharting were read in Neo on public pages. TrendSpider's Neo pages returned ERR_CONNECTION_RESET; the review used the same official pages through the read-only primary-source web tool. Public-page absence of a list is not acceptance. No private application dropdown or Jim session was inspected. The public pages cannot prove an actual payout to Jim.

**One action that starts the test — Jim:** send [the unsent eligibility inquiry](f5-eligibility-inquiry.txt) to TrendSpider's public affiliate-support address. Its official affiliate contract publishes hello@trendspider.com. The query supplies no identity documents, account details or paid commitment. A written answer can resolve the two uncertainties without building anything.

**After an affirmative answer:** Jim handles application/terms and beneficiary details. Publisher then uses one programme's issued link in the existing English chart coverage for 30 days, with a plain affiliate disclosure and separate channel tags. Record clicks, accepted referrals, reversals, earned commissions and cash actually received separately. Proposed continuation rule: at least one verified paid referral and a usable payout route; do not call an accrued balance income received. Zero responses or unresolved eligibility end this programme's test, while the other candidates remain available.

Proposed disclosure: “This is an affiliate link. Capital Chronicle may earn a commission if you subscribe.” This is draft wording for a later approved link, not an inserted promotion. Affiliate membership changes no chart or market-data reuse rights. Keep attribution and the source's actual licence. Prop-firm and broker programmes are excluded by CC-429; none was researched or promoted here.
