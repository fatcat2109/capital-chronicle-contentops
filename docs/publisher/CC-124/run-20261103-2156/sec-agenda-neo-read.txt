SEC MEETINGS AND OTHER EVENTS
Machine-Understandable Data in the Age of Super Intelligence (SI)
Nov 9, 2026
8:00AM – 3:30PM ET
Add to Calendar

The SEC is hosting an event focused on machine‑understandable data and super intelligence (SI). The program will feature panel discussions exploring how market participants use SI and machine‑understandable data, the associated risks and costs, and ways the SEC can further support and enhance the use of these technologies.

Registration for in person attendance is required. A live webcast will be available. Registration is not required to view the webcast.
