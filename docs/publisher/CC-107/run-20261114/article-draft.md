## Scenarios

Capital Chronicle model estimate: our frozen Strong condition required payrolls about +154K or more and unemployment 4.2% or lower. Both conditions held, so this release matched Strong. [[SOURCE:SOURCE_1]]

The frozen Weak alternative required payrolls about -24K or lower and unemployment 4.2% or higher. That combination did not hold. [[SOURCE:SOURCE_1]]

Our frozen Strong mechanism was that a hawkish labour surprise would raise the expected Fed path, the dollar and real yields. The Weak countercase was that another weak print would lower the expected hike path and weaken the dollar. [[SOURCE:SOURCE_1]]

## What the separate score tells us

In the separate historical-frequency comparison, the Capital Chronicle model estimate classified the outcome as in line and assigned the matrix a Brier score of 0.02. Lower is better. [[SOURCE:SOURCE_1]]

This is one event and a different comparison from the preview's Strong and Weak conditions. The in-line category does not reverse which frozen release condition was met or establish predictive skill. [[SOURCE:SOURCE_1]]

For that same event, the Capital Chronicle model estimate gave Investing's consensus-only baseline a Brier score of 0. The consensus baseline therefore performed better on this categorical metric. [[SOURCE:SOURCE_1]]

We cannot count the Strong resolution and the in-line category as independent successes. Neither result supplies a second independent event or a performance record. [[SOURCE:SOURCE_1]]

No prediction-market-only score is available for this event, so we cannot assess that baseline. [[SOURCE:SOURCE_1]]

## The lesson

Our interpretation is that the dollar and gold co-movements are consistent with the frozen hawkish mechanism. The countercase is that consistency does not establish causation, especially without a matched rates observation. [[SOURCE:SOURCE_1]]

What we cannot claim is predictive skill: the consensus-only categorical baseline scored better, and the missing rates and first-day observations leave parts of the mechanism untested. [[SOURCE:SOURCE_1]]

So what: judge the release condition, observed response and forecast baseline separately; agreement between them cannot establish predictive skill. [[SOURCE:SOURCE_1]]

For the next review, we would preserve the frozen conditions and compare fully dated market observations against a baseline before claiming that a mechanism worked. This retrospective provides no current market guidance. [[SOURCE:SOURCE_1]]
