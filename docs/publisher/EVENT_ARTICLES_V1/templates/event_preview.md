This is an editorial brief for the approved writer, not canned reader-facing prose.

conditional reactions and earlier relevant analysis. Raw private dossiers never go
to a public writer. Keep the internal preparation and rights record separate from copy.

| Key | Reader question |

`market_reactions` has exactly `rates`, `dollar`, `gold`, `oil`, `indices` and
`sectors`. Explain conditional mechanisms and competing influences; do not assert
that a conditional reaction has happened. Distinguish Main estimates, prediction
market prices and historical frequencies. Preserve the source's probability measure,
window, uncertainty and incomplete branch coverage; never normalise or invent odds.
Use only source-owned qualitative directions or admitted ranges. No price targets.

Keep the pre-event range separate from entry bounds, stops and targets. A setup's
confidence is not the probability of that economic branch. Never convert either
into the other. If a range or implied move is absent from held sources, omit the
unsupported claim and explain the remaining confirmation test in the article;
retain the exact gap internally. These gaps never hold the supported analysis.
