| Description | **English:** The western front of the [United States Capitol](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/United_States_Capitol). The [Neoclassical style](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Neoclassical_architecture) building is in [Washington, D.C.](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Washington,_D.C.), on [Capitol Hill](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Capitol_Hill,_Washington,_D.C.), at the east end of the [National Mall](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/National_Mall). The Capitol was designated a [National Historic Landmark](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/National_Historic_Landmark) in 1960. |
