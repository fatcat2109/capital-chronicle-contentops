# Can I trade news?

 

FTMO restricts order execution for **2 minutes before and 2 minutes after** selected news releases for all accounts, excluding the exceptions mentioned below. You are allowed to hold open positions on the targeted instruments if they were opened more than 2 minutes before the restricted event. This restriction applies to **any order execution**, including your Stop Loss, Take Profit or any other pending orders. Violating this rule may result in termination of an FTMO Account / FTMO Instant Account.

 

A restriction applies only to symbols that include the affected currency/symbol. Trading on non-targeted instruments is **not restricted**. For example, during the US NFP release, you may trade EURGBP or AUDNZD; however, you must not open or close trades on USDJPY or GBPUSD within the time window of **2 minutes before and 2 minutes after** the NFP release.

 

This restriction does not apply to:

 

 - any **Swing type accounts**
 - any accounts in the **FTMO Challenge or Verification phase**.

 

With these accounts, you can trade during any news freely, provided that the [Forbidden Trading Practices](https://ftmo.com/en/forbidden-trading-practices/) are avoided.

 

The following table lists all restricted events and the affected simulated assets. The news release schedule can also be monitored in our [Economic Calendar](https://ftmo.com/en/calendar/), where these restricted events are marked by the note “**Restricted event**“.

  

| Affected simulated instruments (currency-based) | Macroeconomic announcements |
| ## **USD** - **Forex pairs** with the USD currency involved - **Gold** (XAUUSD) - **US Indices** (US2000.cash, US500.cash, US100.cash, US30.cash) - **DXY** | US announcements: - Federal Funds Rate & Statement - Non-Farm Employment Change - Unemployment Rate & Wages - Advance GDP q/q - FOMC Meeting Minutes - CPI y/y |
| ## **EUR** - **Forex pairs** with the EUR currency involved | EU announcements: - Main Refinancing Rate |
| ## **GBP** - **Forex pairs** with the GBP currency involved | UK announcements: - Official Bank Rate & MPC Votes - CPI y/y |
| ## **CAD** - **Forex pairs** with the CAD currency involved | Canadian announcements: - Overnight Rate/BOC Rate Statement - CPI m/m - Employment Change/Unemployment Rate |
| ## **AUD** - **Forex pairs** with the AUD currency involved | Australian announcements: - Cash Rate & RBA Statement - Employment Change/Unemployment Rate - CPI m/m - CPI y/y - GDP q/q |
| ## **NZD** - **Forex pairs** with the NZD currency involved | New Zealand announcements: - Official Cash Rate & RBNZ Rate Statement - Employment Change/Unemployment Rate - CPI q/q - GDP q/q |
| ## **CHF** - **Forex pairs** with the CHF currency involved | Swiss announcements: - SNB Policy Rate |
