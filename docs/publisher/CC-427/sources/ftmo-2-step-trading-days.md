# FTMO Trading Objectives

 

Understand the key rules that apply to each trading phase and your FTMO Account.

 

## Trading Objectives

 

You must satisfy all applicable Trading Objectives concurrently to meet the Evaluation Process requirements. For FTMO Account, continuous compliance with applicable Trading Objectives is required at all times.

 

The Minimum Trading Days rule requires the trader to achieve at least **4** Trading Days.

 

A Trading Day is defined as any day – **measured from 00:00:00 to 23:59:59 CE(S)T** – during which at least one position is **opened**.

 

This rule applies to both phases (FTMO Challenge and Verification) of the **FTMO Challenge: 2-Step**. There is no Minimum Trading Days rule on the subsequent FTMO Account (2-Step).

 Example 

**Example (account size does not play a role):**

 

 - Position 1: Opened on **12 Jan 2026** and closed on **14 Jan 2026**
 - Position 2: Opened on **18 Jan 2026** and closed on**18 Jan 2026**
 - Position 3: Opened on **18 Jan 2026** and closed on**19 Jan 2026**
 - Position 4: Opened on **24 Jan 2026** and closed on **24 Jan 2026**

 

The Minimum Trading Days rule is not satisfied, as positions were opened only on **3 Trading Days**: **12 Jan**, **18 Jan**, and **24 Jan**. To pass the FTMO Challenge phase or Verification phase, you will have to continue trading one more day.
