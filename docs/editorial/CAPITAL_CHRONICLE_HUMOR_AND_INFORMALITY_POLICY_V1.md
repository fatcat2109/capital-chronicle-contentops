# Capital Chronicle Humor and Informality Policy V1

Authority date: 2026-08-17
Status: `CURRENT_OWNER_TONE_POLICY`
Owner: Jim

Humor is optional. Zero jokes is always valid.

## Purpose

Capital Chronicle may use dry, restrained, financially literate wit to improve memorability and reader retention without weakening authority. Humor must sharpen the mechanism or expose a contradiction; it must never manufacture evidence or convert serious reporting into entertainment.

## Story-class allowance

| Story class | Default allowance |
|---|---|
| Death, casualties, war victims, severe hardship, disaster | Zero |
| Allegations, legal jeopardy, sensitive identity issues | Zero |
| Breaking news | Normally zero |
| Data release | Zero or one dry line |
| Standard economic analysis | Up to one dry line |
| Market mechanism | Up to one or two restrained lines |
| Deep structural analysis | Up to two when they improve comprehension |
| Social derivative | Slightly more informal, same factual limits |

These are ceilings, not quotas.

## Allowed targets

A joke may target:

- a market contradiction;
- a model assumption;
- an institutional incentive;
- a balance-sheet constraint;
- a mismatch between narrative and observable mechanics;
- the speed difference between expectations and physical/economic adjustment.

A joke may not target victims, vulnerable groups, private individuals experiencing hardship, or people at the center of tragedy.

## Style

Prefer deadpan, understatement, clean reversal, or compact irony.

Avoid:

- forced Gen-Z slang;
- all-caps urgency;
- meme catchphrases;
- emoji as punchline infrastructure;
- cheap dunks;
- ideological sneering;
- humor inside primary-evidence quotation or presentation;
- humor that implies buy/sell/hold advice;
- jokes whose premise depends on an unsupported claim.

A strong test is removal: if deleting the joke changes the factual or analytical logic, it was doing work that belongs to evidence or analysis and should be rewritten.

## V2

Spoken/video humor follows the same boundaries. Timing, pause, visual contrast, or music may carry the wit, but the underlying proposition must remain evidence-safe. Generated imagery must not be used to create a joke that could be mistaken for documentary reality.
