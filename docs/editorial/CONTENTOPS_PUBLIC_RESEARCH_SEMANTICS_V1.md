# Public research meaning and uncertainty — editorial consumer V1

Owner direction: 2026-10-21. This is ContentOps' public editorial projection of Main's adopted
question-scoped quant/probability policy (PR237, source `49d875300829215179770097079807ac064c1281`).
It does not change editorial model roles, pause, publication or Main numeric authority.

## Preserve the question through the article

Source fact, Main interpretation, conditional scenario, subjective challenger, statistical
probability and risk-neutral valuation probability remain distinct. A score, model agreement,
review window, seasonal average or option valuation is not a trade win rate. Terminal direction,
barrier-first passage and net-after-cost profit ask different questions. Selection and dependent
copies do not create a new observation or independent confirmation.

The consumer carries exact permitted target/event, instrument/proxy, horizon/cutoff, resolution,
measure/method/evaluation, selection/dependence and limitations where supplied. Missing fields
stay unknown. Useful factual or qualitative journalism does not need a generated probability.
Main alone owns numerical estimation, evaluation and current admission. No new engine, store,
calibrator, status owner or universal article prerequisite is introduced.

## Existing seams

`capital_chronicle_public_research_semantics_v1.py` is a bounded metadata carrier/validator.
The optional `public_research_descriptors` extension is an **editorial consumer contract**,
not a claim that today's Main wire implements it. A descriptor pins its exact report ID/version/
hash and public claim ID/hash; the containing public projection retains ordinary byte/seal/
rights/authority checks. Exact descriptor hash and fields enter the existing claim/source packet.
Current reports without this extension remain supported and labelled UNKNOWN_NOT_SUPPLIED.

Selection/native writing and the one same-writer revision preserve that context. A binding
using a supplied descriptor references its exact fields/hash; validation carries it into the
existing supported-claim lineage. The producer's reader disclosure must be present in the
article and is retained by the existing compiler in all eight derivatives. Writers cannot
self-certify or overwrite this metadata. This grants no new factual/numeric authority and
does not bypass the existing proprietary-numeric/publication gates.

The existing source-review seam treats a changed or removed descriptor as a correction even
when prose and claim IDs remain unchanged. The original article/return/review remains history;
its prepared KEEP cannot be reused until the exact revision is reviewed. Complementary claims
from one release retain distinct component identities, with independence left unestablished.
Natural-language checks are deliberately narrow; exact typed checks and human editorial
reading are complementary, not proof of arbitrary prose entailment.
